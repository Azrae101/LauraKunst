LAURA KRUUSE: WEBSITE

Folders
  index.html         the whole website on one page: prints shop, about me, sketches, coming soon and contact (last)
  museum.html        the only separate page
  auction.html       the auction (hidden for now, see showAuction in js/config.js)
  gallery.html, contact.html, about.html   only send old links on to the right part of the front page
  museum.html        the virtual museum. It hangs every painting in ARTWORKS and every sketch in SKETCHES automatically
  css/style.css      all styling
  js/config.js       settings: MobilePay number, e-mail, links, paintings, prices for shipping
  js/i18n.js         all text in Danish and English
  js/site.js         basket, checkout, auction and language logic
  images/general/    photos of Laura, the Japper studio logo, favicons, the print frame and the museum guests
  images/paintings/  the paintings and sketches
  supabase-setup.sql the database

Getting it live
  1. Make a free project on supabase.com. Open the SQL Editor, paste supabase-setup.sql,
     change the dates and prices at the bottom, and click Run.
  2. In Supabase, open Project settings, then API. Copy the Project URL and the anon key
     into js/config.js.
  3. Make a free form on formspree.io and paste its address into js/config.js.
  4. Fill in the MobilePay number, e-mail and Messenger link in js/config.js.
  5. Upload the whole folder to Netlify or GitHub Pages.

Until step 3 is done, the site runs in demo mode and only saves bids and orders in your own browser.

Changing text
  Fixed text is in js/i18n.js. Every line has a Danish (da) and an English (en) version.
  Painting descriptions and the coming soon items are in js/config.js.

Adding a new painting
  1. Save the picture as a .jpg in images/paintings, around 1200 to 1600 pixels tall.
     In js/config.js its image is then "images/paintings/name.jpg".
  2. Add it to ARTWORKS in js/config.js with its width (w) and height (h) in pixels.
     The site uses those numbers to show the picture at its own shape without cropping.
  3. Add a row for it in Supabase (auctions and prints tables) with the same id.

After changing a .css or .js file
  Browsers keep old copies of css and js files for up to 10 minutes. In index.html, museum.html
  and auction.html, change the number after ?v= (for example ?v=20261124 to ?v=20261125) on
  the style.css, config.js, i18n.js and site.js lines. Then every visitor gets the new files at once.
